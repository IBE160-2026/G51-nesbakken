Not sent. User can adapt this for Newtown Graffiti Map and the Inner West Council Perfect Match team. Ask the recipient to identify the right copyright holders if they cannot grant the rights themselves.

Hello,

We are building Jaunt, a student walking-tour app initially covering Newtown. We would like to show photographs from your public-art gallery inside each artwork’s information panel, with clear photographer, artist and source credits.

Could you confirm whether you can grant permission for the photographs and the artworks they depict, or direct us to the relevant rights holders? We would like to host copies in the app, show them in a publicly accessible student prototype, and resize them proportionally for phone screens. We also hope to expand the app later, so please state any limits on future use, duration, distribution or commercial use. If an existing open licence applies, please send its exact terms.

For each approved image, please identify the image/file, photographer, artwork artist, required attribution and source link, and confirm whether the permission covers both the photograph and the depicted artwork.

Potential Newtown Graffiti Map subjects: I Have a Dream, Promise, Housing Bubble, Making All Things New, Bring Back the Butterflies and Sydney Park Hotel.

Potential Council subjects: Characters of Newtown, The Spirit of St Peters, Bring Back the Butterflies, Sydney Park Hotel, Nature Always Wins and Familiar Form.

We will only add the specific images whose permissions we can verify. Thank you.
